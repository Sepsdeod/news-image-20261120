A full-day front-page poster summing up the world's news of Friday
20 November 2026: six stories, each verified against same-day
reporting (Reuters, AP). Drawn from scratch with Pillow (`draw.py`);
`main.ts` copies the finished `entry.png` to the output directory,
byte for byte, every run.

## The six stories

1. **Russia strikes Kyiv bridges; missiles enter Poland** — An
   overnight barrage kills nine across Ukraine and damages three
   Kyiv bridges; two Russian missiles and a jet-powered drone enter
   Polish airspace, which Warsaw calls a deliberate provocation.
   (Reuters, 20 November 2026)
2. **Mexico captures "El Chapo Isidro"** — Mexican special forces
   seize Fausto Isidro Meza Flores, an FBI Top 10 fugitive, in
   Guasave, Sinaloa, on Friday night; cartel gunmen answer with
   burning roadblocks. (Reuters / AP, reported 21 November of the
   Friday-night operation)
3. **Stocks plunge into correction** — The S&P 500 falls 3.7% to
   6,910.39, a seventh straight loss; Nvidia dives about 14% after
   Reuters reports the SEC is examining its accounting. (Reuters,
   20 November 2026)
4. **UK and EU seal post-Brexit deals** — Food trade, linked carbon
   markets and youth mobility are agreed at the second EU–UK summit
   in Brussels. (20 November 2026)
5. **COP31 overruns its deadline** — The climate talks in Antalya
   pass their Friday close with no deal; the latest draft drops
   every mention of fossil fuels and the $120bn adaptation-finance
   goal. (20 November 2026)
6. **Senate passes permitting bill 62–33** — A sweeping energy
   permitting overhaul clears the US Senate and heads to the House;
   the chamber also rejects an Iran war-powers resolution 51–48.
   (Reuters, 20 November 2026)
