One poster, six stories: the world news of Friday 20 November 2026,
drawn as a front page. Every story on it was checked against reporting
from that day (Reuters, AP). The picture is painted from scratch by
`draw.py` with Pillow primitives — no photos, no borrowed artwork —
and `main.ts` copies the finished `entry.png` to the output folder,
byte for byte, on every run.

## What happened on 20 November 2026

1. **Russia hits Kyiv bridges in a night barrage** — Nine people are
   killed across Ukraine and three Kyiv bridges are damaged; two
   Russian missiles and a jet-powered drone cross into Polish
   airspace, which Warsaw calls a deliberate provocation. (Reuters,
   20 November 2026)
2. **Mexico seizes "El Chapo Isidro"** — Mexican special forces take
   Fausto Isidro Meza Flores, an FBI Top 10 fugitive, in Guasave,
   Sinaloa, on Friday night; cartel gunmen answer with burning
   roadblocks. (Reuters / AP, reported 21 November of the
   Friday-night operation)
3. **Stocks slide into correction** — The S&P 500 drops 3.7% to
   6,910.39, its seventh straight loss; Nvidia falls about 14%
   after Reuters reports the SEC is examining its accounting.
   (Reuters, 20 November 2026)
4. **UK and EU agree a post-Brexit pact** — Food trade, linked
   carbon markets and youth mobility are settled at the second
   EU–UK summit in Brussels. (20 November 2026)
5. **COP31 talks miss their deadline** — The climate talks in
   Antalya pass their Friday close with no deal; the newest draft
   leaves out fossil fuels and the $120bn adaptation-finance goal.
   (20 November 2026)
6. **Senate clears the permitting bill 62–33** — A sweeping energy
   permitting overhaul passes the US Senate and heads to the House;
   the chamber also votes down an Iran war-powers resolution 51–48.
   (Reuters, 20 November 2026)
